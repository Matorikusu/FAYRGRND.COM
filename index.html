FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about the protocol.

How does verification stop fake accounts? +
FAYRGRND uses a hybrid protocol combining AI verification with peer-assisted ledger consensus. Each individual can claim only one unique profile on the network.
What determines my Star Rating and Honour Level? +
Your reputation is determined by feedback from real-world Link-Ups, completed achievements, and community interactions within Crews.
Can bad actors alter their status artificially? +
No. All rating shifts require peer-verified validation recorded on the immutable ledger, preventing sybil attacks or artificially inflated stats.
